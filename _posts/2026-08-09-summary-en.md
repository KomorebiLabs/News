## [Generative Design of Novel Bacteriophages Using Genome Language Models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate whole-genome sequences based on the ΦX174 bacteriophage template, experimentally validating 16 viable, evolutionarily novel phages — the first successful generative design of functional bacteriophage genomes. This breakthrough demonstrates that frontier genome language models can generate functional whole genomes with realistic genetic architectures and desired host tropism, marking a major step forward for AI-driven synthetic biology and the design of biological systems at scale. The study used the lytic phage ΦX174 as a design template; Evo 2 is a 40-billion-parameter DNA foundation model trained on 9 trillion base pairs with a 1 million base pair context length, capable of single-nucleotide resolution modeling.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Bacteriophages, or phages, are viruses that infect bacteria and have been studied since the early 20th century. ΦX174 was the first DNA genome to be sequenced and the first to be chemically synthesized. Genome language models like Evo 2 are deep learning models trained on massive genomic datasets to predict and design biological sequences, analogous to how language models like GPT handle text.
