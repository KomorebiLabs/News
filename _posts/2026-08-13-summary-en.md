## [Google Announces Gemini 3.7 Flash with Introductory Pricing](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google has announced Gemini 3.7 Flash, its latest fast-thinking model, with introductory pricing that will double on December 31, 2026. The model is positioned as a cost-effective option for high-volume text and vision tasks. This release intensifies competition in the affordable LLM market, especially as models like GPT-5.6 Luna offer aggressive discounts. It gives developers a new option for cost-sensitive deployments while pushing rivals to adjust their pricing strategies. The model's pricing will double on December 31, 2026, with rates reaching $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens from January 1, 2027. Community testing highlights strong vision-to-code performance, though Opus 5 still leads in image-to-HTML accuracy.
