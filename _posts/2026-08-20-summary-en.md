## [Terence Tao warns AI could cause math's biggest crisis since Gödel](https://the-decoder.com/terence-tao-says-ai-could-trigger-maths-biggest-crisis-since-godel/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

In his 2026 ICM article, Terence Tao warns that AI-generated mathematical proofs could lead to a crisis of 'proof overload,' where too many results exist for humans to verify or understand. He compares this potential crisis to the foundational crises triggered by Russell's paradox and Gödel's incompleteness theorems in the early 20th century. This warning is significant because it highlights a potential shift in mathematical research from proof scarcity to proof abundance, challenging the traditional human-centric verification process and raising questions about the nature of mathematical understanding. The First-Proof project's second round tested 10 unpublished research problems on four AI systems, with seven passing at least one system, each costing tens to hundreds of dollars. Tao argues that even formally verified proofs should be considered incomplete if no human can clearly explain them.
