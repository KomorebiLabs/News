## [Yangtze Memory's STAR Market IPO Accepted, Seeking 33 Billion Yuan](https://api3.cls.cn/share/article/2461025?os=android&amp;sv=8.8.2&amp;app=cailianpress) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Shanghai Stock Exchange has accepted Yangtze Memory Technologies' STAR Market IPO application, with a planned fundraising of 33 billion yuan. The company, which recently entered the global top 3 NAND flash manufacturers, reported Q1 2026 revenue of 470.42 billion yuan and net profit of 333.79 billion yuan. This is a major milestone for China's semiconductor industry, as Yangtze Memory is the country's leading NAND flash manufacturer. The IPO will strengthen domestic memory chip production capacity and reduce reliance on foreign suppliers in an increasingly competitive global semiconductor landscape. The IPO sponsors are CITIC Securities and CITIC Construction Bank. The company's IPO tutoring period lasted approximately three months, ending with 辅导验收 on August 19. According to Counterpoint data, Yangtze Memory ranked in the global top 3 NAND market by shipment volume in Q2 2026.

**Background**: The STAR Market (科创板) is a trading platform on the Shanghai Stock Exchange established in 2019, focusing on high-tech and strategically emerging companies. It uses a registration-based IPO system that streamlines the listing process for innovative firms. NAND flash memory is a type of non-volatile storage technology widely used in SSDs, USB drives, and memory cards, and it retains data without power.
