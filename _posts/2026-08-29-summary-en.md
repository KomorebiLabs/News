## [SPC Algorithm Beats SOTA on Time Series Anomaly Detection Benchmark](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1w1wt1s/you_can_beat_sota_time_series_anomaly_detection/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Eamonn Keogh argues that the TSB-AD benchmark is too trivial, as a 100-year-old Statistical Process Control algorithm can match or exceed state-of-the-art methods on it. This critique challenges the validity of widely-used benchmarks in time series anomaly detection research, raising questions about whether recent progress is meaningful or merely benchmark overfitting. Keogh demonstrated that SPC achieves perfect results on certain ECG traces from the TSB-AD benchmark, and he has prepared more challenging datasets including sled dogs, tuna, fuel cells, and smart manufacturing data.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/eamonnkeogh · Aug 29, 20:16

**Background**: The TSB-AD benchmark is a large-scale dataset for time series anomaly detection, introduced at NeurIPS 2024, containing 1070 high-quality time series from 40 datasets. Statistical Process Control (SPC) is a collection of statistical tools for monitoring and controlling processes, developed in the early 20th century, commonly used in industrial quality control.
