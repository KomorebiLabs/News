## [Anthropic Formalizes Fermat's Last Theorem in 13-Million-Line Lean Repository](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic has formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean, producing a 13-million-line repository containing 29,500 intermediate theorems. This marks a major milestone in AI-assisted mathematical verification and formal methods. This achievement demonstrates that large-scale formal verification is now feasible, potentially catching errors in the common body of mathematical proofs and reducing the burden of refereeing new work. It represents a significant step forward for AI-assisted mathematics and the broader formal methods ecosystem. The formalization uses the Darmon–Diamond–Taylor 1995 exposition of the Wiles–Taylor–Wiles argument, developing Fontaine theory and Mazur's work on the Eisenstein ideal. Notably, this is not the modern proof that Kevin Buzzard has been formalizing himself.

hackernews · jlebar · Sep 4, 18:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49568506)

**Background**: Fermat's Last Theorem states that no three positive integers a, b, and c satisfy the equation a^n + b^n = c^n for any integer n > 2. Andrew Wiles proved it in 1994 using deep connections between elliptic curves and modular forms. Formal verification uses proof assistants like Lean to write mathematically rigorous proofs that can be mechanically checked for correctness, eliminating human error.

**Discussion**: The community is enthusiastic about the milestone, with Kevin Buzzard providing context on what the accomplishment means. Some commenters note the proof uses the Darmon–Diamond–Taylor exposition rather than the modern approach Buzzard himself has been formalizing. Terence Tao's concern about proofs becoming too complex for humans to understand resonates with the 13-million-line scale of this work.

## [GPT-6 Released, OpenAI Claims Entry into AGI Era](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1w6v0ig/gpt6_is_released_n/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has released GPT-6, with benchmark results showing it scores around 60% on ARC-AGI-3 without a harness and greatly exceeds the human baseline on GDPval-AA v2. OpenAI President Greg Brockman stated that it is not unreasonable to feel we are now in the AGI era. This release marks a significant milestone in AI capabilities, potentially signaling the transition to artificial general intelligence and raising profound questions about the future of work and economic displacement. GPT-6 achieves approximately 60% on ARC-AGI-3 without a harness, while on GDPval-AA v2 it joins a growing list of models that greatly exceed human baseline across 44 occupations and 9 industries. ARC-AGI-3 is an interactive reasoning benchmark designed to measure human-like intelligence.

**Background**: ARC-AGI-3 is the first interactive reasoning benchmark that challenges AI agents to explore novel environments and acquire goals on the fly, measuring human-like intelligence rather than narrow task performance. GDPval-AA v2 is a composite benchmark aggregating nine challenging evaluations across mathematics, science, coding, and reasoning, with Elo ratings anchored to human-expert performance across real-world knowledge-work deliverables.
