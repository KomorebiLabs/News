## [Asahi Linux Announces Official M3 Support](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Asahi Linux has officially announced support for M3, M3 Pro, and M3 Max chips, expanding its reach to more Apple Silicon desktops. This milestone advances the Linux-on-Apple-Silicon ecosystem, enabling more users to run Linux natively on Apple's latest hardware. While M3 support is a major step, adoption blockers remain, including poor llama.cpp performance compared to Metal, lack of sleep and HDMI support, and Apple's continued withholding of hardware specifications.

hackernews · mdp2021 · Sep 6, 14:08 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586698)

**Background**: Asahi Linux is an open-source project that reverse-engineers Apple Silicon hardware to run Linux on Apple Macs. When Apple transitioned from Intel processors to its custom ARM-based M-series chips, the Linux community faced a challenge due to lack of documentation. The project develops drivers and tools, with its flagship distro being Fedora Asahi Remix.

## [NVIDIA Releases DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering](https://t.me/zaihuapd/43632) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NVIDIA has officially released DLSS 5, introducing 3D-guided neural rendering that generates more realistic lighting and materials in real-time. The technology launches on September 3rd with NBA 2K27, supporting GeForce RTX 50 Series PCs, laptops, and GeForce NOW Ultimate cloud gaming. This represents a major evolution in NVIDIA's upscaling technology, shifting from traditional upscaling to generative neural rendering that can enhance visual fidelity while maintaining performance. It will impact PC gamers and cloud gaming subscribers who want photorealistic graphics without requiring prohibitively expensive hardware. DLSS 5 uses learned appearance priors while preserving developer-authored content and design intent. On RTX 5090, it achieves up to 370 FPS at 4K with ultra settings and ray tracing, and 590 FPS at 1440p. The neural model semantically interprets completed color frames using motion information to modify lighting and materials.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 6, 03:20

**Background**: DLSS (Deep Learning Super Sampling) is NVIDIA's AI-powered upscaling technology that has evolved through multiple versions, improving gaming performance by rendering at lower resolutions and upscaling with neural networks. DLSS 5 introduces a new paradigm where instead of merely upscaling, the system uses generative neural rendering to add photorealistic lighting and material details that enhance the base rendered image while respecting the game's original art direction.
